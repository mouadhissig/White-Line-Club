About Us

Who We Are

White Line Club is a dynamic student organization at L'Institut Supérieur de Sciences Infirmières Gabès, dedicated to nurturing the next generation of healthcare leaders.

Founded with a vision to bridge academic excellence with practical healthcare experience, we provide a platform for nursing students to develop their skills, engage with the community, and prepare for impactful careers in healthcare.

Our members are passionate individuals committed to making a difference in the healthcare sector, driven by values of compassion, integrity, and continuous learning.
